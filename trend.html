The rule, in plain English

  1. Each trading day, take SPY's last 200 daily closing prices and average them.
  2. If the latest close is above that average, the market's long-term trend is up: BUY / HOLD_LONG.
  3. If it is at or below the average, the trend has broken: DEFENSIVE_CASH / EXIT, to protect against big drawdowns.
  4. The rule is advisory. It places no orders; any trade still goes through your approval and risk limits.
